CellGuard

Protect your MacBook battery health with custom lower/upper charge limits, native AC pass-through holding, low-battery alerts, automatic Battery Saving Mode, and built-in one-click updates.

Download CellGuard v1.0.1 (.dmg)

First-Time Install (30 seconds)

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag CellGuard into Applications.
  2. Clear macOS download quarantine once in Terminal:
    xattr -cr /Applications/CellGuard.app
  3. Launch CellGuard, click the shield icon in the menu bar, and click Install once to enable hardware charge limits.
